## 🔍 **Responsible Disclosure Program**

### **Our Commitment**

DataSphere Guilds believes in working with the security community to protect our users and maintain the integrity of our platform. We encourage responsible disclosure of security vulnerabilities and are committed to working with researchers to address issues promptly and effectively.

### **What We Promise**

- **No Legal Action**: We will not pursue legal action against researchers who follow our responsible disclosure guidelines
- **Safe Harbor Protection**: Complete legal protection for good-faith security research
- **Rapid Response**: Acknowledgment within 24 hours, initial assessment within 72 hours
- **Transparent Communication**: Regular updates throughout the remediation process
- **Public Recognition**: Credit in our security hall of fame (with your permission)
- **Financial Rewards**: Monetary compensation through our bug bounty program

### **Guidelines for Researchers**

#### ✅ **Encouraged Activities**
- Testing on your own accounts or with explicit permission
- Automated scanning with reasonable request rates (<10 requests/second)
- Social engineering of DataSphere Guilds employees (with prior coordination)
- Physical security testing of our offices (with advance notice)
- Analysis of open-source components and public repositories

#### ❌ **Prohibited Activities**
- Testing on accounts you don't own without explicit permission
- Accessing, modifying, or deleting user data
- Degrading or disabling our services (DoS/DDoS attacks)
- Phishing or social engineering attacks against our users
- Testing third-party services that integrate with our platform
- Violating applicable laws or regulations

### **Disclosure Process**

1. **Initial Report**: Submit via security@datasphereguilds.com with detailed information
2. **Acknowledgment**: Receive confirmation within 24 hours with case ID
3. **Assessment**: Our security team evaluates the report within 72 hours
4. **Coordination**: Work together on remediation timeline and disclosure plan
5. **Resolution**: Receive confirmation when the vulnerability is fixed
6. **Public Disclosure**: Coordinated public disclosure (typically 90 days after fix)

## 💰 **Bug Bounty Program**

### **Program Overview**

Our bug bounty program rewards security researchers for responsibly disclosing vulnerabilities that could impact the security, privacy, or integrity of DataSphere Guilds platform and infrastructure.

### **Reward Structure**

| Severity | Impact | Reward Range | Examples |
|----------|--------|--------------|----------|
| **Critical** | Complete system compromise | $10,000 - $50,000 | RCE, Authentication bypass, Financial fraud |
| **High** | Significant data exposure | $2,500 - $10,000 | Data injection, Privilege escalation, Payment manipulation |
| **Medium** | Limited security impact | $500 - $2,500 | Information disclosure, Session management flaws |
| **Low** | Minimal security risk | $100 - $500 | Missing security headers, Minor information leaks |

### **Bonus Multipliers**

- **🎯 Premium Targets**: 2x rewards for mobile app vulnerabilities
- **🚀 Novel Techniques**: 1.5x for innovative attack vectors
- **🔧 Exploitation Proof**: 1.5x for working proof-of-concept

### **Premium Bounty Targets**

| Component | Base Multiplier | Special Focus |

### **Eligibility Requirements**

## ⏱️ **Response Process & SLAs**

### **Response Timeline Commitments**

| Severity | Acknowledgment | Initial Assessment | First Update | Resolution Target |
|----------|----------------|-------------------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Critical** | 2 hours | 4 hours | 24 hours | 7 days |
| **High** | 12 hours | 24 hours | 72 hours | 30 days |
| **Medium** | 24 hours | 72 hours | 1 week | 90 days |
| **Low** | 72 hours | 1 week | 2 weeks | 180 days |

### **Detailed Response Process**

#### **Phase 1: Intake & Triage (0-24 hours)**
1. **Automated Acknowledgment**: Immediate email confirmation with case ID
2. **Initial Triage**: Security team reviews and categorizes the report
3. **Severity Assignment**: Preliminary severity rating based on impact assessment
4. **Team Assignment**: Assign to appropriate security specialist or team

#### **Phase 2: Investigation & Assessment (1-7 days)**
1. **Technical Validation**: Reproduce and verify the reported vulnerability
2. **Impact Analysis**: Assess potential business and technical impact
3. **Affected Systems**: Identify all systems and components affected
4. **Exploitability Review**: Evaluate ease of exploitation and attack vectors

#### **Phase 3: Response & Remediation (1-90 days)**
1. **Immediate Mitigation**: Deploy temporary fixes or protections
2. **Root Cause Analysis**: Identify underlying causes and systemic issues
3. **Permanent Fix Development**: Engineer comprehensive solution
4. **Testing & Validation**: Thorough testing in staging environments

#### **Phase 4: Deployment & Verification (1-7 days)**
1. **Production Deployment**: Roll out fixes using safe deployment practices
2. **Verification Testing**: Confirm vulnerability is properly addressed
3. **Monitoring Enhancement**: Implement additional monitoring if needed
4. **Documentation Update**: Update security procedures and documentation

#### **Phase 5: Communication & Closure (1-30 days)**
1. **Researcher Notification**: Inform reporter of successful remediation
2. **Public Disclosure Coordination**: Plan coordinated public disclosure
3. **Internal Communication**: Brief relevant stakeholders and teams
4. **Process Improvement**: Identify lessons learned and process improvements

### **Emergency Response Protocol**

#### **Critical Security Incidents**
- **Immediate Response Team**: CISO, Lead Security Engineer, Engineering Manager
- **Emergency Contact**: security-urgent@datasphereguilds.com

## 🏗️ **Security Architecture**

### **Defense in Depth Strategy**

#### **Layer 1: Edge Protection**
- **WAF (Web Application Firewall)**: Cloudflare Enterprise with custom rules
- **DDoS Protection**: Multi-vector attack mitigation up to 100 Tbps
- **Rate Limiting**: Intelligent rate limiting with behavioral analysis
- **Geo-blocking**: Country-specific access controls for high-risk regions
- **Bot Protection**: Advanced bot detection and mitigation

#### **Layer 2: Network Security**
- **VPC Architecture**: Isolated virtual private clouds with strict segmentation
- **Network ACLs**: Granular network access control lists
- **VPN Access**: Zero-trust VPN for all administrative access
- **Network Monitoring**: Real-time network traffic analysis and anomaly detection
- **Intrusion Detection**: IDS/IPS with machine learning-based threat detection

#### **Layer 3: Application Security**
- **Secure SDLC**: Security integrated throughout development lifecycle
- **Static Analysis**: Automated code scanning (SonarQube, Veracode)
- **Dynamic Testing**: Runtime application security testing (DAST)
- **Dependency Scanning**: Continuous monitoring of third-party dependencies
- **Security Headers**: Comprehensive security header implementation

#### **Layer 4: Data Protection**
- **Encryption at Rest**: AES-256 encryption for all stored data
- **Encryption in Transit**: TLS 1.3 for all communications
- **Key Management**: Hardware Security Modules (HSM) for key storage
- **Data Classification**: Automated data discovery and classification
- **Data Loss Prevention**: DLP controls for sensitive data protection

#### **Layer 5: Identity & Access**
- **Zero Trust Architecture**: Never trust, always verify approach
- **Multi-Factor Authentication**: Required for all user accounts
- **Privileged Access Management**: Just-in-time access for administrative functions
- **Identity Federation**: SAML/OIDC integration with enterprise identity providers
- **Continuous Authentication**: Behavioral and risk-based authentication

### **Infrastructure Security**

#### **Cloud Security (AWS)**
- **IAM Policies**: Least-privilege access with regular access reviews
- **VPC Configuration**: Private subnets, NAT gateways, security groups
- **Secrets Management**: AWS Secrets Manager with automatic rotation
- **Compliance**: SOC 2, ISO 27001, FedRAMP compliance
- **Monitoring**: CloudTrail, GuardDuty, Security Hub integration

#### **Container Security**
- **Image Scanning**: Vulnerability scanning for all container images
- **Runtime Protection**: Container runtime security monitoring
- **Secrets Injection**: Secure secret injection at runtime
- **Network Policies**: Kubernetes network policies for pod communication
- **Admission Control**: OPA/Gatekeeper for policy enforcement

#### **Database Security**
- **Encryption**: Transparent Data Encryption (TDE) for all databases
- **Access Control**: Role-based database access with regular audits
- **Query Monitoring**: Real-time SQL injection detection
- **Backup Security**: Encrypted backups with immutable storage
- **Data Masking**: Dynamic data masking for non-production environments

### **Mobile Application Security**

#### **iOS Security**
- **Certificate Pinning**: SSL certificate pinning for API communications
- **Keychain Services**: Secure storage using iOS Keychain
- **App Transport Security**: ATS enforcement for all network connections
- **Code Obfuscation**: Binary protection against reverse engineering
- **Runtime Protection**: Anti-tampering and anti-debugging measures

#### **Android Security**

#### **Responsible Testing Guidelines**

2. **Limit Impact**: Minimize potential impact to production systems
3. **Respect Privacy**: Do not access other users' data
4. **Document Thoroughly**: Provide detailed reproduction steps
5. **Use Latest Versions**: Test against current production versions
6. **Follow Disclosure Timeline**: Adhere to coordinated disclosure practices

```markdown
## Vulnerability Summary
Brief description of the vulnerability and its impact

## Affected Assets
List of affected systems, applications, or components

Technical details of the vulnerability

Step-by-step instructions to reproduce the issue

## Impact Assessment
Potential impact and risk to users/business

Screenshots, videos, or code demonstrating the vulnerability

Suggested fixes or mitigation strategies
```
