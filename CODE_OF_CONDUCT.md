## Executive Summary

DataSphere Guilds is committed to fostering a professional, inclusive, and trustworthy global marketplace where workers, inspectors, developers, and organizations can collaborate effectively in data collection and quality assurance activities. This Code of Conduct establishes the behavioral standards, ethical guidelines, and enforcement mechanisms necessary to maintain the integrity, safety, and productivity of our platform.

**Key Principles:**
- **Integrity**: All interactions must be honest, transparent, and ethical
- **Respect**: Every community member deserves dignified treatment
- **Fairness**: Decisions and evaluations must be impartial and evidence-based
- **Accountability**: All parties are responsible for their actions and their consequences
- **Excellence**: We strive for the highest standards in all aspects of our work

## Our Commitment

DataSphere Guilds pledges to create and maintain an environment where:

### 1. **Diversity & Inclusion**
- We welcome participants from all backgrounds, cultures, countries, and demographics
- We actively promote equal opportunities regardless of race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, religion, disability, age, or socioeconomic status
- We recognize and celebrate the value that diverse perspectives bring to our global community

### 2. **Professional Excellence**
- We maintain the highest standards of data quality, accuracy, and reliability
- We foster continuous learning and professional development
- We encourage innovation while maintaining ethical boundaries

### 3. **Trust & Safety**
- We protect the privacy and personal information of all community members
- We maintain secure systems and processes for financial transactions
- We provide multiple channels for reporting concerns and seeking resolution

### 4. **Transparency & Accountability**
- We clearly communicate our policies, procedures, and decision-making processes
- We provide regular updates on platform improvements and policy changes
- We take responsibility for our actions and their impact on the community

## Core Values & Principles

### 1. **Integrity in All Actions**

**Professional Honesty**
- Provide accurate and truthful information in all submissions and communications
- Acknowledge limitations in knowledge, skills, or capabilities
- Report conflicts of interest or potential biases that may affect work quality
- Maintain confidentiality of proprietary information and trade secrets

**Data Accuracy**
- Ensure all data collection follows specified guidelines and requirements
- Report technical issues or anomalies that may affect data quality
- Verify information accuracy before submission when possible
- Maintain detailed documentation of data collection methodologies

**Financial Integrity**
- Accurately report time spent on tasks and expenses incurred
- Avoid fraudulent activities including fake accounts, bot usage, or collusion
- Promptly report payment errors or discrepancies
- Comply with tax obligations and reporting requirements in applicable jurisdictions

### 2. **Respect for All Community Members**

**Interpersonal Respect**
- Treat all community members with dignity, courtesy, and professionalism
- Listen actively to different perspectives and viewpoints
- Provide constructive feedback that focuses on work quality, not personal characteristics
- Acknowledge contributions and achievements of others

**Cultural Sensitivity**
- Respect diverse cultural backgrounds, traditions, and practices
- Avoid assumptions based on stereotypes or prejudices
- Adapt communication styles to be inclusive and accessible
- Recognize that humor, colloquialisms, and cultural references may not translate across cultures

**Professional Boundaries**
- Maintain appropriate professional relationships with all community members
- Respect privacy and personal information of others
- Avoid requesting or sharing personal contact information outside official channels
- Recognize and respect different time zones and work schedules

### 3. **Fairness in Evaluation and Decision-Making**

**Objective Assessment**
- Base all evaluations on clearly defined criteria and evidence
- Avoid bias based on personal relationships, demographics, or unrelated factors
- Provide specific, actionable feedback to support improvement
- Maintain consistency in evaluation standards across similar tasks

**Transparent Processes**
- Clearly communicate evaluation criteria and expectations
- Provide rationale for decisions when requested
- Maintain audit trails for significant decisions
- Ensure equal access to opportunities and resources

**Conflict Resolution**
- Address disagreements professionally and constructively
- Seek to understand all perspectives before making judgments
- Utilize established appeal and mediation processes when appropriate
- Focus on finding mutually beneficial solutions

### 4. **Commitment to Excellence**

**Quality Standards**
- Strive to exceed minimum requirements whenever possible
- Continuously improve skills and knowledge through learning opportunities
- Share best practices and lessons learned with the community
- Maintain high standards even under pressure or tight deadlines

**Innovation and Improvement**
- Suggest improvements to processes, tools, and methodologies
- Embrace new technologies and approaches that enhance quality
- Collaborate effectively to solve complex problems
- Maintain a growth mindset and openness to feedback

## Platform-Specific Guidelines

### 1. **Task Performance Standards**

**Data Collection Workers**
- **Accuracy Requirements**: Maintain a minimum 95% accuracy rate across all submitted tasks
- **Timeliness**: Complete tasks within specified deadlines unless granted extensions
- **Completeness**: Ensure all required fields and deliverables are provided
- **Quality Standards**: Follow detailed task instructions and quality checklists
- **Documentation**: Maintain detailed logs of data collection methodologies and sources
- **Ethics**: Obtain necessary permissions when collecting data from individuals or private property
- **Safety**: Prioritize personal safety and report dangerous conditions immediately

**Quality Control Inspectors**
- **Impartiality**: Evaluate submissions based solely on objective criteria and evidence
- **Consistency**: Apply evaluation standards uniformly across all submissions
- **Thoroughness**: Review all aspects of submissions according to established protocols
- **Timeliness**: Complete reviews within specified timeframes
- **Documentation**: Provide detailed feedback and rationale for all decisions
- **Continuous Improvement**: Regularly calibrate evaluation standards with peer reviewers
- **Confidentiality**: Protect sensitive information encountered during reviews

**Appeal Reviewers**
- **Objectivity**: Evaluate appeals based on evidence and established criteria
- **Fairness**: Provide all parties with equal opportunity to present their case
- **Thoroughness**: Investigate all relevant aspects of disputed decisions
- **Transparency**: Clearly communicate decision rationale and supporting evidence
- **Consistency**: Apply similar standards to similar cases
- **Timeliness**: Resolve appeals within established timeframes
- **Documentation**: Maintain comprehensive records of appeal processes and outcomes

### 2. **Quality Assurance Protocols**

**Submission Guidelines**
- **Original Work**: All submissions must be original work performed by the submitter
- **Accuracy Verification**: Verify accuracy of all data before submission
- **Completeness Check**: Ensure all required components are included
- **Quality Control**: Perform self-review against provided checklists
- **Metadata**: Include all required metadata and documentation
- **Version Control**: Use appropriate version control for iterative tasks
- **Backup**: Maintain backup copies of all work until final approval

**Review Standards**
- **Comprehensive Evaluation**: Review all aspects of submissions systematically
- **Evidence-Based Decisions**: Base all decisions on documented evidence
- **Constructive Feedback**: Provide specific, actionable feedback for improvements
- **Consistency**: Apply evaluation criteria uniformly across all submissions
- **Calibration**: Regularly align evaluation standards with other reviewers
- **Documentation**: Maintain detailed records of review processes and decisions
- **Continuous Improvement**: Regularly update review criteria based on feedback and results

**Appeal Process**
- **Timely Submission**: Submit appeals within specified timeframes
- **Clear Documentation**: Provide clear explanation of grounds for appeal
- **Supporting Evidence**: Include all relevant supporting documentation
- **Professional Conduct**: Maintain respectful tone throughout appeal process
- **Compliance**: Follow all established appeal procedures and requirements
- **Good Faith**: Submit appeals only when there are legitimate grounds for dispute
- **Acceptance**: Accept final decisions gracefully and learn from feedback

### 3. **Financial Transaction Standards**

**Payment Processing**
- **Accuracy**: Ensure all payment information is accurate and up-to-date
- **Timeliness**: Process payments according to established schedules
- **Transparency**: Provide clear documentation of all payment calculations
- **Compliance**: Adhere to all applicable tax and regulatory requirements
- **Security**: Protect all financial information and maintain secure processes
- **Dispute Resolution**: Follow established procedures for payment disputes
- **Record Keeping**: Maintain detailed records of all financial transactions

**Fraud Prevention**
- **Identity Verification**: Verify identity of all parties involved in financial transactions
- **Activity Monitoring**: Monitor for suspicious patterns or activities
- **Reporting**: Report suspected fraudulent activities immediately
- **Cooperation**: Cooperate fully with fraud investigations
- **Prevention**: Implement and maintain robust fraud prevention measures
- **Education**: Educate community members about fraud risks and prevention
- **Recovery**: Implement procedures for recovering from fraud incidents

## Prohibited Conduct

The following behaviors are strictly prohibited and may result in immediate suspension or permanent removal from the platform:

### 1. **Harassment and Discrimination**

**Direct Harassment**
- Threats of violence or physical harm against any individual
- Stalking, doxxing, or sharing personal information without consent
- Persistent unwanted contact after requests to stop
- Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual advances
- Intimidation or bullying behaviors
- Revenge actions against individuals who report violations

**Discriminatory Behavior**
- Discrimination based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age, or other protected characteristics
- Use of slurs, epithets, or derogatory language targeting protected groups
- Exclusion or differential treatment based on protected characteristics
- Creating hostile environments for members of protected groups
- Promoting supremacist ideologies or hate speech

**Systemic Harassment**
- Coordinated harassment campaigns against individuals or groups
- Creating multiple accounts to harass specific individuals
- Encouraging others to engage in harassment activities
- Using platform features to systematically exclude or target individuals
- Manipulating systems to unfairly disadvantage specific users

### 2. **Fraudulent Activities**

**Platform Fraud**
- Creating false accounts or impersonating others
- Manipulating ratings, reviews, or feedback systems
- Collusion between workers and inspectors to obtain unfair advantages
- Submitting plagiarized or falsified work
- Using automated systems or bots to complete tasks requiring human judgment
- Providing false information about qualifications, experience, or credentials

**Financial Fraud**
- Payment fraud including chargebacks, stolen payment methods, or money laundering
- Tax evasion or failure to report income as required by law
- Creating false invoices or expense claims
- Manipulating payment systems to obtain unauthorized funds
- Participating in pyramid schemes or other illegal financial activities

**Data Fraud**
- Falsifying data collection results or methodologies
- Submitting synthetic or artificially generated data as authentic
- Misrepresenting data sources or collection methods
- Violating data usage agreements or licensing terms
- Selling or sharing proprietary data without authorization

### 3. **Abuse of Platform Systems**

**Technical Abuse**
- Hacking, cracking, or unauthorized access to platform systems
- Distributing malware, viruses, or other malicious software
- Attempting to bypass security measures or access controls
- Exploiting system vulnerabilities for personal gain
- Interfering with platform operations or other users' access

**System Manipulation**
- Gaming algorithms or ranking systems through artificial means
- Creating artificial scarcity or demand for tasks
- Manipulating task assignments or distribution mechanisms
- Interfering with quality control processes
- Exploiting appeals or dispute resolution systems

**Resource Abuse**
- Excessive use of platform resources or bandwidth
- Spamming or flooding communication channels
- Creating unnecessary system load through inefficient practices
- Hoarding tasks or resources to disadvantage other users
- Wasting platform resources through negligent or malicious behavior

### 4. **Violation of Legal and Ethical Standards**

**Legal Violations**
- Violating applicable laws and regulations in any jurisdiction
- Infringing on intellectual property rights
- Violating privacy laws and data protection regulations
- Engaging in illegal activities using platform resources
- Facilitating or enabling illegal activities by others

**Ethical Violations**
- Violating professional codes of conduct or ethical standards
- Engaging in conflicts of interest without proper disclosure
- Misusing confidential or proprietary information
- Violating research ethics or human subjects protections
- Engaging in unfair competition or business practices

**Regulatory Violations**
- Violating industry-specific regulations or standards
- Failing to comply with licensing or certification requirements
- Violating export controls or international trade restrictions
- Engaging in activities that violate platform compliance obligations
- Failing to report required information to regulatory authorities

## Investigation Process

### 1. **Initial Assessment**

**Intake and Triage**
- All reports are acknowledged within 24 hours
- Initial assessment of severity and urgency
- Assignment to appropriate investigation team
- Determination of immediate protective measures needed
- Communication with reporting party about next steps

**Preliminary Review**
- Gathering of immediately available evidence
- Identification of all parties involved
- Assessment of potential policy violations
- Determination of investigation scope and timeline
- Coordination with relevant stakeholders

### 2. **Investigation Procedures**

**Evidence Collection**
- Systematic collection of all relevant evidence
- Preservation of digital evidence and communications
- Interviews with all relevant parties
- Review of relevant policies and procedures
- Analysis of patterns and trends in similar cases

**Due Process**
- All parties are informed of their rights and responsibilities
- Opportunity for all parties to present their perspective
- Access to representatives or advocates when appropriate
- Fair and impartial investigation procedures
- Regular updates on investigation progress

**Expert Consultation**
- Involvement of subject matter experts when needed
- Legal counsel consultation for complex cases
- External investigators for cases involving senior management
- Technical experts for system-related issues
- Cultural consultants for cross-cultural issues

### 3. **Investigation Standards**

**Objectivity and Impartiality**
- Investigators have no conflicts of interest
- All evidence is considered fairly and equally
- Decisions are based on preponderance of evidence
- Bias and prejudice are actively avoided
- Regular training for investigation teams

**Timeliness**
- Investigations are completed within reasonable timeframes
- Regular updates are provided to all parties
- Delays are communicated promptly with explanations
- Urgent cases receive priority attention
- Appeals and reviews are handled expeditiously

**Thoroughness**
- All relevant evidence is collected and analyzed
- All parties have opportunity to present their case
- Alternative explanations are considered
- Corroborating evidence is sought when possible
- Comprehensive documentation is maintained

## Consequences & Remedial Actions

### 1. **Progressive Discipline Framework**

**Level 1: Verbal Warning**
- **Applicable to**: Minor first-time violations, misunderstandings
- **Actions**: Discussion with supervisor or moderator, clarification of expectations
- **Documentation**: Record of conversation and expectations
- **Follow-up**: Check-in within 30 days to ensure compliance
- **Appeal Rights**: Right to request review of warning

**Level 2: Written Warning**
- **Applicable to**: Repeated minor violations, moderate first-time violations
- **Actions**: Formal written notice, mandatory training or education
- **Documentation**: Written record in personnel file
- **Follow-up**: 60-day probationary period with enhanced monitoring
- **Appeal Rights**: Right to formal appeal process

**Level 3: Suspension**
- **Applicable to**: Serious violations, repeated moderate violations
- **Actions**: Temporary suspension from platform (1-30 days)
- **Documentation**: Comprehensive investigation report
- **Follow-up**: Mandatory retraining before reinstatement
- **Appeal Rights**: Right to formal appeal with external review

**Level 4: Permanent Removal**
- **Applicable to**: Severe violations, violations causing significant harm
- **Actions**: Permanent ban from platform, account termination
- **Documentation**: Full investigation file and decision rationale
- **Follow-up**: Ongoing monitoring to prevent circumvention
- **Appeal Rights**: Right to one final appeal with independent arbitration

### 2. **Restorative Justice Measures**

**Victim-Centered Remedies**
- Compensation for financial losses or damages
- Public or private apologies when appropriate
- Removal of harmful content or communications
- Counseling and support services
- Protective measures to prevent future harm

**Community Restoration**
- Community service or contribution requirements
- Public education or awareness activities
- Mentoring or coaching responsibilities
- Participation in community improvement projects
- Sharing of lessons learned with others

**Systemic Improvements**
- Policy updates to prevent similar violations
- System enhancements to detect and prevent problems
- Training programs for community members
- Process improvements for reporting and resolution
- Regular reviews and updates of procedures

### 3. **Mitigating and Aggravating Factors**

**Mitigating Factors**
- First-time violation with no prior history
- Immediate acknowledgment and acceptance of responsibility
- Voluntary disclosure of violations
- Cooperation with investigation processes
- Demonstrated commitment to making amends

**Aggravating Factors**
- Repeat violations or pattern of misconduct
- Deliberate or malicious intent
- Significant harm to individuals or community
- Obstruction of investigation or appeals processes
- Leadership position or special responsibility

**Special Circumstances**
- Mental health or disability considerations
- Cultural or language barriers
- Coercion or duress situations
- Systemic or organizational failures
- Emergency or crisis situations

## Appeals & Due Process

### 1. **Appeal Rights and Procedures**

**Who Can Appeal**
- Any party directly affected by an enforcement decision
- Reporting parties who believe resolution is inadequate
- Community members affected by systemic issues
- Representatives acting on behalf of affected parties
- Advocacy groups in cases of broad community impact

**Appeal Timelines**
- Appeals must be submitted within 30 days of decision notification
- Emergency appeals can be submitted immediately for urgent situations
- Extensions may be granted for good cause
- Appeal hearings are scheduled within 60 days of submission
- Decisions are rendered within 30 days of hearing completion

**Appeal Procedures**
- Written appeal submission with specific grounds and evidence
- Assignment to independent appeal panel
- Opportunity for oral hearing if requested
- Right to representation or advocacy
- Comprehensive review of original decision and evidence

### 2. **Appeal Review Standards**

**Grounds for Appeal**
- Procedural errors in original investigation or decision
- New evidence that was not available during original review
- Disproportionate or inappropriate sanctions
- Bias or conflict of interest in original decision-makers
- Violation of due process rights

**Review Process**
- Independent review panel with no involvement in original decision
- Fresh examination of all evidence and circumstances
- Consideration of mitigating and aggravating factors
- Application of current policies and standards
- Consultation with relevant experts and stakeholders

**Decision Authority**
- Appeal panels have authority to affirm, modify, or reverse decisions
- Ability to order additional investigation or remediation
- Authority to modify sanctions or remedies
- Power to recommend policy or procedural changes
- Final decision authority except for cases involving legal issues

### 3. **Final Review and Arbitration**

**External Arbitration**
- Available for cases involving permanent suspension or removal
- Independent arbitrators with relevant expertise
- Binding arbitration with limited judicial review
- Costs shared between parties unless otherwise determined
- Expedited process for urgent cases

**Legal Review**
- Legal counsel review for cases involving potential legal violations
- Coordination with law enforcement when appropriate
- Compliance with court orders and legal requirements
- Protection of legal rights and interests
- Privileged communication protections

**Board Review**
- Board-level review for cases involving systemic issues
- Policy review and recommendation authority
- Oversight of enforcement consistency and effectiveness
- Authorization for major policy changes
- Final authority for organizational decisions

**Continuing Education**
- Regular review of updated policies and procedures
- Participation in required training and education programs
- Staying informed about changes in laws and regulations
- Seeking clarification when policies are unclear
- Continuous improvement of knowledge and skills

**Active Participation**
- Positive contribution to community culture and values
- Support for other community members
- Participation in governance and improvement processes
- Advocacy for ethical behavior and high standards
- Leadership by example in all interactions

**Responsibility for Others**
- Intervention when witnessing violations or harm
- Mentoring and education of new community members
- Creating inclusive and welcoming environments
- Supporting victims of violations or harm
- Promoting ethical behavior throughout the community

### 3. **Modification and Updates**

**Policy Updates**
- Regular review and updating of policies and procedures
- Community consultation on significant changes
- Clear communication of policy updates
- Reasonable notice periods for implementation
- Grandfathering provisions for existing situations

**Community Input**
- Regular solicitation of community feedback
- Formal consultation processes for major changes
- Community representation in governance processes
- Transparent decision-making procedures
- Regular reporting on policy effectiveness

**Continuous Improvement**
- Regular assessment of policy effectiveness
- Benchmarking against industry best practices
- Integration of lessons learned from incidents
- Adaptation to changing legal and regulatory requirements
- Innovation in enforcement and prevention approaches
